Privacy Policy — PULSE Reaction Arena

Effective date: September 20, 2026

PULSE Reaction Arena ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. It is a reaction-time game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Game data. Your reaction scores, game settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers.

How We Use Information

Game data stored on your device is used only to run the game (tracking scores and preferences). We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to any third party.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no account data for us to show, correct, or delete.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
