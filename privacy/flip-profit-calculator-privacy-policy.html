Privacy Policy — Flip Profit Calculator

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Flip Profit Calculator ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. It is a deep-sea adventure game with 30 levels. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Game data. Your game progress, level completion, scores, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers.

How We Use Information

Game data stored on your device is used only to run the game (tracking progress, scores, and preferences). We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to any third party.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. The game content is loaded from our servers to deliver the game; no personal information is collected in the process.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no account data for us to show, correct, or delete. You can clear locally stored game data at any time through your device's app settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
