Privacy Policy — Utility Hub: Essential Tools

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Utility Hub: Essential Tools ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. It is a collection of everyday utility tools, including a QR code scanner, calculators, and converters. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Information you enter into the tools. Anything you type into the app's tools (for example, values you convert or calculate) is processed on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store it on any server.

Camera. The QR code scanner uses your device's camera to read codes in real time. Camera images are analyzed on your device solely to detect QR codes — they are not saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.

Purchase information. The optional Pro upgrade is purchased through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

How We Use Information

Information entered into the app is used only to provide the tool's function on your device. We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to third parties except as described under Advertising below.

Advertising

This app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve, personalize, and measure ads.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization). For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no account data for us to show, correct, or delete. Advertising choices are controlled through your device's ad settings and Google's Ad Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
